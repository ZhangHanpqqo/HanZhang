(<e>)(2025) - Theegma duo

Intermedia theater performance with real-time visual projection, no_input_dev, and participatory mobile device live-streaming.

In the theatre, where do the sights rest? The intermedia composition casts the question back to the audience with the loudness of a lasting gaze.

Theegma duo is a cross-media performance duo between Han Zhang and Yifan Guo.

Presented in Chaospace, DiMenna Center, NYC, as part of the DramaEtMusica 2025 Edition.
